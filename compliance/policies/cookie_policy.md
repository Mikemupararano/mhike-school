# Cookie Policy
**Last updated:** [Insert Date]

This Cookie Policy explains how Mhike School (“we”, “our”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you use our platform.

---

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and improve user experience.

---

## 2. How We Use Cookies

Mhike School uses **minimal cookies and storage mechanisms**, strictly for essential functionality.

### 2.1 Essential Authentication
We use:
- Session-based storage (e.g. `sessionStorage`)
- Authentication tokens (JWT)

These are required to:
- Keep users logged in
- Maintain secure sessions
- Authorise access to protected routes

Without these, the platform will not function.

---

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

| Type            | Purpose                         | Required |
|-----------------|---------------------------------|----------|
| Essential       | Authentication & session        | ✅ Yes   |
| Functional      | UI preferences (future use)     | ⚠️ Limited |
| Analytics       | Platform usage insights         | ❌ No (not currently used) |
| Marketing       | Advertising / tracking          | ❌ No |

---

## 4. What We DO NOT Use

We explicitly do **not** use:
- Third-party advertising cookies
- Tracking cookies for marketing
- Cross-site tracking tools

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

We may rely on infrastructure providers (e.g. hosting, databases), but:
- They do **not** set cookies on your device through our platform
- They process data only as processors under strict agreements

---

## 6. Managing Cookies

Because we only use essential cookies:

- Disabling cookies may break login and authentication
- Most browsers allow you to:
  - Block cookies
  - Clear stored data
  - Control site permissions

---

## 7. Legal Basis

Under GDPR, essential cookies are processed under:
- **Legitimate interest** (secure platform operation)
- **Contractual necessity** (providing the service)

Consent is not required for essential cookies.

We may update this policy if:
- New features require cookies
