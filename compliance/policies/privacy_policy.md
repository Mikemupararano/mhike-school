## 2. What Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Full name
- Email address
- Role (student, teacher, school admin, platform admin)
- School affiliation (school_id)

### 2.2 Authentication Data
- Encrypted passwords (hashed, never stored in plaintext)
- Login attempts and timestamps
- Access tokens (JWT)

### 2.3 Educational Data
- Courses and content accessed
- Assignments and submissions
- Grades and feedback

### 2.4 System & Audit Data
- Audit logs (e.g. account changes, role updates)
- Activity logs (e.g. content creation, submissions)
- IP address (if logged for security purposes)

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We process personal data for the following purposes:

- To authenticate users and provide secure access
- To deliver educational services (courses, assignments, grading)
- To manage schools and user roles
- To enforce permissions and access control
- To monitor platform usage and improve performance
- To ensure security, prevent fraud, and investigate misuse
- To comply with legal obligations

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not sell personal data**.

We may share data with:
- Schools (for users belonging to that school)
- Service providers (e.g. hosting, database providers)
- Legal authorities when required by law

All third-party processors are required to implement appropriate security measures.

---

## 6. Data Storage & Security

We implement strong technical and organisational measures:

- Passwords are securely hashed
- JWT-based authentication for access control
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- School-level data isolation (multi-tenant separation)
- Secure database storage (PostgreSQL)
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
